What is Centurio Private?

Six months of one on one coaching across your mind, your body and the way you perform, built for you from your own Mental Fitness Score. It holds twelve deep sessions of ninety minutes, twelve accountability calls, your training and nutrition programmed alongside it, WhatsApp access to me through the week, and a half day in person at the start. There is no set curriculum. The programme is built around what you actually need.

How does it work week to week?

Every fortnight there is a ninety minute session where we work whichever capacity is live, and an accountability call in between to hold you to what came out of it. Between those, you are running one practice, training to a programme and eating to a plan, and I am on WhatsApp when something comes up. Every month ends with one practice you are genuinely running rather than one you have agreed to.

What is the Mental Fitness Assessment, and why does everything start there?

The Mental Fitness Assessment is thirty six questions across six Core Capacities, six questions each, giving you a number on every one. It tells us where the work actually is, which is almost never where a person assumes it is. You take it at the start, again at month three and again at the end, so we are steering by something real the whole way through.

How much time does it take?

Around three hours a month of contact with me, plus the training and the practices, which fold into your week instead of sitting on top of it. The deep sessions are the only fixed commitment. Everyone who has done this work arrives expecting it to cost them time and finds it gives time back, because most of what gets rebuilt is the hours you were already losing.

Why six months?

Shorter than that and you get insight. Six months is long enough to work all six capacities properly, long enough for the habits to break and get rebuilt once or twice, and long enough that the person at the end has evidence rather than enthusiasm. Twenty six weeks of promises kept to yourself is the thing that rewrites who you think you are, and it cannot be hurried.

My schedule is unpredictable and I travel. Does that work?

Yes. The sessions are booked around you and they move when they need to. The whole model was built for people whose weeks look nothing alike, which is why the daily work is small and portable and the heavy lifting happens in sessions you choose the timing of.

I am already performing. Is this for me?

That is who it is built for. The people who get the most out of six months are the ones already operating at a level, who can feel there is another one available and have run out of ways to reach it on their own. Starting from a strong base means we spend the time on the capacities holding you back instead of the basics.

What qualifies you to coach me?

I built the Centurio model, and every practice inside it I run on myself. Twenty years of building and operating businesses sits behind it, and the six capacities came out of working out what actually held when things got hard. The honest answer is that you will know within twenty minutes on the call whether I am the right person to spend six months with, and that is the correct way to decide something like this.

Could I get the same thing from the Centurio platform?

The platform gives you the model, the Score, the training and a check in every week, and you apply all of it yourself. Plenty of people get a long way with it and that is exactly what it is there for. Private is six months of me applying it to you, building the programme around your numbers, sitting in the hard conversations, and holding you to the thing you said you would do when the week goes sideways. Every Private client has the platform too.
