How it works

USE THE NUMBER.
THEN USE THE FEEDBACK.

The calculator creates a starting target. Your real response over several weeks tells you whether the target is serving the goal.

What the calculator uses

Energy. Resting energy is estimated from age, height, body weight and sex, then multiplied by your selected activity level.

Protein. Set in grams per kilogram using either current or desired body weight.

Fat. Set as a percentage of daily calories.

Carbohydrate. Receives the calories remaining after protein and fat are allocated.

Member practice
Choose a target. Follow it consistently. Review body weight, energy, training performance and recovery. Make small changes from evidence rather than from one unusual day.
This tool is for general adult education and planning. It is not medical nutrition therapy and is not a substitute for individual advice from a qualified health professional.
