OPTIONAL ADD ON

The Four Week Follow Through.

One day of training changes what people know. Four weeks of practice changes what they do. The Follow Through extends the program across your whole group for the month after the session.

  • A weekly briefing, delivered to every participant
  • Weekly check in questions tied to what the group trained
  • A retake of the 36 question assessment at the end of week four, so the change is measured

Applied across everyone who attended, so the whole team moves together.
