Why this exists

To make Mental Fitness as normal as Physical Fitness.

You already own half of that. You have spent years teaching people how to train a body, and you are very good at it.

The other half is the part nobody trained you for. It is also the part that decides whether your client is still standing in front of you in March.

We did not invent the exercises. We built the gym for your mind to train them.

"I believe we have a responsibility to become all that we are capable of becoming."
Sterling Cincotta, Founder, Centurio

Sterling hosts every session himself, live. The Centurio framework is built on the work of Simon Sinek, James Clear, Tim Grover, Shade Zahrai, Maya Raichoora and Tony Robbins, and he coaches it one on one every week.

The problem you already know

You have watched this happen all year.

They start strong. Six weeks in, they are a different person.

Then work gets loud. Something at home goes wrong.

They miss a Tuesday. Then the Thursday.

They stop replying to your messages.

And you lose a client who was doing really well.

Nothing went wrong with the programming. The programming was fine. What broke was the promise they made to themselves, and the moment that breaks, you are not getting them back with a better split.

That is a trainable capacity. It has a method, it has evidence behind it, and in forty five minutes you will be able to use it on Tuesday morning.

Who this is for

Trainers who already care past the session.

Come along if

  • You are an independent trainer or you run a small studio.
  • You already think about your clients between sessions.
  • You are tired of losing good people to life getting busy.
  • You want something to offer that nobody down the road has.

Probably not for you if

  • You are looking for a qualification to put on a wall.
  • You want a done for you program with no learning in it.
  • You are after clinical or psychological training. This is coaching, and anyone who needs clinical help gets referred on.

Trainers employed by a chain are very welcome. Come and learn, and take the referral link.
