Why It Happens

It is not motivation. It is a skill nobody taught them.

Of 5,240 gym members tracked through a full lifecycle, 63% stopped training before the third month. Fewer than four in a hundred were still going at twelve.

Visit frequency is the strongest predictor there is. A member who attends fewer than four times in their first month has an 80% chance of cancelling.

Underneath that number is self efficacy, a person's belief in their own ability to follow through. It is one of the most studied predictors of exercise adherence, and it sits alongside self regulation and resilience.

All three are trainable. None of them are trained in your session, because your session is sixty minutes long and there is a body in front of you.

Sperandei, Vieira and Reis, Journal of Science and Medicine in Sport, 2016, n = 5,240. Plus published gym retention and exercise adherence research.
