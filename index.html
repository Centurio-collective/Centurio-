DEFINITION

Mental Fitness is the lifelong practice of strengthening the six Core Capacities that help us continually become all that we are capable of becoming.

THE MODEL

Six permanent capacities. Trained every week, for life.

One capacity, one Tuesday. The cycle repeats, the practice never finishes.

Lead
Trust Yourself

The ability to consistently lead yourself through integrity, discipline and responsibility.

Align
Know Your Direction

Align your actions with your values and purpose.

Regulate
Master Your State

Manage your thoughts, emotions and states. Respond intentionally rather than react impulsively.

Connect
Elevate Others

Build trust through genuine relationships. Listen deeply, communicate honestly and contribute generously.

Grow
Remain Teachable

Commit to lifelong learning and continual improvement. Stay curious, adaptable and open to change.

Perform
Turn Intention Into Action

Execute consistently when it matters most. Turn intention into disciplined action.
