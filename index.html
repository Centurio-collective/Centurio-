OUR METHOD

Progressive Capacity Theory

Your mind takes load the way your body does. Six capacities, a number on each, and the lowest one is where your training starts. A small daily load, a little heavier every week, and a measurement at the end that tells you whether it worked.

Motivation makes it possible. Reps make it inevitable.
