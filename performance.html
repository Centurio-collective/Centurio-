Every engagement starts the same way. We work out what you are actually training for, we measure where you are, and we build the programme backwards from the thing you want to be true.

Training is written for your body, your schedule, your equipment and your history, and it changes as you change. Nutrition sits underneath it, calculated rather than guessed, built around food you will genuinely eat.

Then there is the part nobody else is coaching. You take the Mental Fitness Check and we find which of the six capacities is holding the rest down. That one gets trained alongside the body, every week, for as long as we work together.

Everything is quoted individually, because a sixteen year old swimmer with a national qualifier in March and a forty five year old founder who has not trained since his second child need different things from me.

Every programme works. The difference is who is running it.

The programme is the easy part. It can be written in an afternoon and there are a thousand good ones.

What decides whether you are still running it in March is the part of you that gets up when it is cold and nothing hurts enough to make you. Professionals lose it. Beginners have never built it. It is trainable, and almost nobody trains it.

So that is what gets trained here, alongside your body. Centurio measures six capacities, finds the one holding the rest down, and loads it a little heavier every week, for a month at a time. That is how a season of training turns into a life of it.
