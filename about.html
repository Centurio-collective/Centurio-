ABOUT US

I believe we have a responsibility to become all that we are capable of becoming.

I did not always believe it. For a long stretch I would have told you I was doing fine and I would have meant it, because the businesses were working, the numbers were going the right way, and nobody looking in from the outside could see a problem.

Here is what the inside looked like.

I was 103 kilos. I ate whatever was quickest and I drank most nights, and the drinking had stopped being a decision some years before that. I gambled. I made promises to myself on a Sunday evening and broke them by Tuesday, then made them again the following Sunday, which is its own particular kind of exhausting. My state ran the room. Something would go against me at nine in the morning and everyone near me knew about it by ten, and I would lose the rest of the week to a thing that took ninety seconds to happen.

None of that appears in a set of accounts. It appears in the people around you, in what you are like at home, and in the quiet certainty that there is a version of you somewhere that you are not currently being.

I have spent a great deal of money becoming the person I am today. Some of it went on courses, coaches, programmes and books. The best money I have ever spent. Far more of it went on decisions I made while I was not in control of myself, and those were the expensive ones. I paid for this education the hard way, and not in course fees.

I remember the day I had enough.

It was time to take massive action. What I understood standing there was that massive action was going to look like something very small. A few adjustments, made that day, then made again the next day, and the day after that, for as long as it took to stay on the path while everything underneath me changed.

It took time. It was always going to take time. The decision happened in a moment and the transformation happened in increments, and I have been making them ever since.

What I could not find was anywhere to train it.

I could book a session for my body. I could walk into any gym in the country, and somebody would measure me, give me a programme and hold me to it, and nobody would think that was strange. My mind was running every part of my life and there was nothing. No measure, no programme, no gym. Advice, yes. Plenty of advice. Nothing you could turn up to on a Tuesday and do the reps.

So I built it.

The six Core Capacities are not something I read in a book and decided to sell. They are what it actually took, named afterwards. Every one of them is a thing I got wrong first.

Lead is the promises you keep to yourself, and I had broken every one of mine. Align is knowing where you are going, and I had been busy for years without being able to say what it was adding up to. Regulate is your state, and mine had been running me since I was old enough to have one. Connect is the people around you, and the people closest to me were getting the worst of me. Grow is staying teachable, and I had known most of this for a decade without changing any of it. Perform is turning intention into action, and my intentions were excellent.

Six things. Train them and your life changes. Leave them and it does not.

Today I am alcohol free. I do not bet. I train, I eat to a plan, and I take the same assessment I give everybody else, because the work does not finish and I am not above my own programme.

That is not a before and after story. It is a practice, and I am still in it.

Mental Fitness should be as normal as Physical Fitness.

That is what Centurio is for. Not as a slogan, as a thing that should be literally true, with a measure, a programme, a coach, and a room you turn up to.

Business owners, executives and athletes who are performing and know with total certainty that there is another level sitting there.

And people in the position I was in. If you are running something successful and the thing you are not talking about is the drinking, or the betting, or the fact that you have not kept a promise to yourself in two years, I know exactly what that is from the inside and nothing you say is going to shock me.

One thing I will always be straight about. I am a coach and Centurio is training, not treatment. If what you are carrying needs medical or psychological care, I will tell you that on the call and I will help you find it, and that is not me handing you off. A lot of people need both. What I do is the part that comes after the decision, which is building the person who can keep it.
