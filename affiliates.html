The strongest predictor of cancellation

Visit frequency is the single strongest predictor of whether a member cancels. Members who attend fewer than four times in their first month have an 80% chance of cancelling. Visit frequency is a psychological variable expressed as behaviour, discipline, consistency, and belief in your own ability to follow through.

The evidence

Self efficacy, a person's belief in their own ability to follow through, is one of the most studied predictors of exercise adherence, consistently linked to how well people set goals, manage setbacks, and push through the moments that make most people quit.

Self regulation works alongside it. Research on executive function shows the capacity to manage impulses and stay on a chosen path predicts whether someone actually shows up, independent of motivation alone.

Psychological resilience closes the loop. People with higher resilience sustain exercise adherence for longer, and the relationship runs both directions. Training resilience supports consistency, and consistency builds resilience. These are trainable, measurable psychological skills. That is the entire premise Centurio is built on.
