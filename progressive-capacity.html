Our method

Progressive Capacity Theory™

The training method behind everything we build.

Your mind takes load the way your body does.

Your body gets stronger when you add a little load, let it adapt, then add a little more. It works whether you believe in it or not, which is why nobody argues about it.

Your mind takes load the same way. The reason nobody has trained it like this before is that nobody could measure it. You could not say which part of you was weakest. You could not say whether anything you did had made a difference.

So we measure it. Six capacities, a number on each. The lowest number is the one holding the other five down, and that is where your training starts. You carry a small daily load on that one capacity, a little heavier each week, for a month. Then you measure again.

Motivation makes it possible. Reps make it inevitable.

Six capacities. One of them is holding the rest down.
